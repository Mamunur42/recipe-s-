Lasagna Recipe

Home

Description

This is a simple and delicious lasagna recipe that everyone will love!

It's very healthy and easy to make.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and drain the excess fat.
  3. Add the marinara sauce to the skillet and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  5. Spread a thin layer of the meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  6. Layer half of the cooked noodles, then half of the ricotta cheese, and then half of the mozzarella cheese.
  7. Repeat the layers once more.
  8. Top with the remaining Parmesan cheese.
  9. Bake for 30 minutes or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
