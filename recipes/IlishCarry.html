Ilish Carry Recipe

Home

Description

This is a simple and delicious Ilish Carry recipe that everyone will love!

It's a popular dish in Bangladesh and is known for its rich flavors and aromatic spices.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Ilish fish
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 inch ginger, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice

Steps

  1. Heat the cooking oil in a pan.
  2. Add the chopped onion and sauté until golden brown.
  3. Add the minced garlic and ginger, and cook for another minute.
  4. Add the cumin powder, coriander powder, turmeric powder, and red chili powder. Cook for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the Ilish fish and cook until it's done.
  6. Stir in the yogurt and lemon juice.
