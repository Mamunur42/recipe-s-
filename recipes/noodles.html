Noodles Recipe

Home

Description

This is a simple and delicious noodles recipe that everyone will love!

It's very healthy and easy to make.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of noodles
  • 1 cup of water
  • 1 tablespoon of oil
  • 1 teaspoon of salt

Steps

  1. Bring the water to a boil in a large pot.
  2. Add the noodles and cook according to the package instructions.
  3. Drain the noodles and return them to the pot.
  4. Add the oil and salt, and mix well.
